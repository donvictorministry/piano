About the app

DV-Piano Architecture

DV-Piano is an ultra-low-latency acoustic piano instrument engine designed exclusively for Android touchscreen devices and developed for the Don Victor Ministries Online Community & the body of Christ. It contains a dedicated library of hymns, gospel songs and Christmas carols.

The entire sound synthesis pipeline operates locally within the Android environment using mathematical oscillators through the Web Audio API. No audio samples are downloaded from remote servers, ensuring fully offline operation.

System Standards

Built with 100% zero Content Delivery Network dependencies, strict compliance with mobile viewport dimensions, and accessibility guidelines enforcing that no typography is displayed below DV design convention

Equipped with an advanced dynamics compressor to eliminate speaker distortion, lookahead algorithmic sequencing, and multi-timbre synthesis.

How to use

1. Touch & Glissando

Touch any key to produce instant sound. Slide across keys for a continuous sweep.

2. Octave Shift

Use -1 OCT, C4, +1 OCT to transpose the full keyboard.

3. Sustain Pedal

PEDAL ON lets notes ring naturally after release.

4. Instrument Timbre

Switch between Grand Piano, Electric Rhodes, Gospel Organ, Poly Synth.

5. Recorder

REC records your playing with a live timer. REPLAY plays it back.

6. Follow-Me Tutor

TUTOR ON + pick a hymn — the correct key lights amber until you play it.

7. Chord Mode

CHORD ON triggers full triads from a single touch.

8. Reverb

REVERB ON adds in-browser hall reverberation.

9. Volume, Scale & BPM

Adjust VOL, dim non-scale keys with Scale, and set tempo with the BPM field.
