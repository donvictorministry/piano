About the app

DV-Piano Architecture

DV-Piano is an ultra-low-latency acoustic piano instrument engine designed exclusively for Android touchscreen devices and developed for the Don Victor Ministries Online Community.

The entire sound synthesis pipeline operates locally within the Android environment using mathematical oscillators through the Web Audio API. No audio samples are downloaded from remote servers, ensuring fully offline operation.

System Standards

Built with 100% zero Content Delivery Network dependencies, strict compliance with mobile viewport dimensions, and accessibility guidelines enforcing that no typography is displayed below DV design convention 0.

Equipped with an advanced dynamics compressor to eliminate speaker distortion, lookahead algorithmic sequencing, and multi-timbre synthesis.

How to use DV-Piano & Instruments

1. Manual Touch & Glissando

Touch any white or black piano key to produce instant sound. Slide your finger across keys to play a continuous glissando sweep smoothly.

2. Octave Shift (-1, Normal, +1)

Use the -1 OCT, C4, and +1 OCT buttons to transpose the entire keyboard across lower bass registers or higher treble octaves.

3. Sustain Damper Pedal

Tap PEDAL to toggle acoustic damper resonance. When ON, notes ring out naturally after your finger lifts from the keys.

4. Sound Timbre Selector

Switch between Grand Piano, Electric Rhodes, Gospel Organ, and Poly Synth timbres directly from the top features bar.

5. Performance Recorder

Tap ● REC to record your live playing. Tap STOP REC when done, then tap ▶ REPLAY to listen to your recorded performance.

6. Interactive Follow-Me Tutor

Turn TUTOR: ON and pick any song. The piano illuminates the target key in amber and waits for you to strike the correct key before advancing!

7. One-Touch Triad Chord Mode

Toggle CHORD: ON to trigger complete harmonic three-note chords with a single finger touch.
